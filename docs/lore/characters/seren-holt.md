# Seren Holt

**Status:** Working canon

Seren is an aging veteran in his mid-to-late sixties. He was a young captain in Grayhaven's city guard during the siege, responsible for the defense of the Lower Ward. His strength is no longer youthful power; it is the endurance and discipline of someone who has spent his life refusing to yield.

## The siege

After the tide gate fell, priests working under Orra applied the ward that would carry the Bell's protection across Grayhaven. Seren knew the refugees sheltering in the cathedral would bear the cost. His soldiers barred the cathedral doors and prevented anyone from leaving while Orra performed the ritual.

The ritual saved Seren from a mortal wound during the final assault. He is alive because someone inside the cathedral received that wound in his place.

After the battle, Seren helped seal the cathedral and signed the official account claiming the refugees had died in the flood. Grayhaven remembers him as one of the heroes who saved the city. Seren knows that reputation rests on a deliberate sacrifice and the lie used to conceal it.

Seren began drinking to sleep after the siege and eventually became dependent on alcohol. His drinking is an attempt to suppress his memories and guilt, not evidence that he has already decided to confess or seek redemption.

## The oathbreaker

Seren had sworn to protect those who sheltered behind Grayhaven's walls. He broke that oath when he chose the city over the refugees in the cathedral. The title describes what Seren believes himself to be, not how the public remembers him.

## What Seren knows

Seren knows the refugees were sacrificed, the bell preserved the defenders, and the city falsified the history of the siege. He does not know why the victims became undead, what Orra did after the battle, or what breaking the binding will do to its beneficiaries.

His route through the adventure is therefore not about discovering that Grayhaven committed a crime. It is about confronting the full consequences of a crime he helped commit.

## Why he returns

Seren has no commission to enter the cathedral. When the bell begins to toll, pain returns to the place where his mortal wound vanished. No scar remains. He cannot tell whether the bell is calling him or whether guilt is drawing him back, but he feels compelled to descend.

That compulsion does not determine what Seren will do below. He may preserve the binding, expose the crime, free the dead, or act from self-preservation. The choice belongs to the player.

The city watch knows Seren by reputation, and its senior officers know the honored account of his actions during the siege. That gives him a plausible way to persuade the guards at the Drowned Stair without granting him Riona's automatic access.

## Unresolved

- Seren's exact age and formal rank during the siege.
- The nature and location of the mortal wound the ritual took from him.
- Whether Ilyra represented the refugees and received Seren's promise that the cathedral would protect them. This relationship is promising but not yet established canon.
