# Brother Cael

**Status:** Working canon

Cael is an excommunicated priest and former church archivist. People outside the church still call him Brother Cael, although the church no longer recognizes the title. His public accusation that Saint Orra's miracle had been falsified earned him the name "the Heretic."

## The altered history

While cataloguing records from the siege, Cael found burial rolls naming refugees who sheltered in the cathedral but never appeared on Grayhaven's memorials. He later found an older copy of the Bell of Mercy's funerary rite with passages missing from subsequent editions.

Cael raised the discrepancies privately and was ordered to stop. He continued his research and eventually accused the church in public of altering the history of the miracle. He was excommunicated, but retained copies of the records he had found.

## Sister Mara

Sister Mara was Cael's superior in the church archives. She ordered him to end his investigation and later gave evidence during the proceedings that led to his excommunication. Cael sees her as someone who chose the institution over the truth. Mara knows that exposing the truth carries consequences Cael does not yet understand.

## What Cael knows

Cael knows refugees were removed from the official record, the Bell's rite was altered or suppressed, and senior clergy deliberately obstructed his investigation. He does not know that fatal wounds were transferred into the cathedral, why the refugees became undead, what Orra did after the battle, or what will happen if the binding is broken.

Cael genuinely wants the truth exposed, but he also wants proof that he was right and the church was wrong. The player determines whether he ultimately values the victims, his own vindication, his remaining faith, or Grayhaven's survival most.

## Access

Opening the Drowned Stair gives Cael his first opportunity to search the cathedral itself. The church has warned the city watch not to admit him, making a direct approach harder for Cael than for the other protagonists.

## Unresolved

- Cael's age, order, and life before he entered the church archives.
- The exact contents of the missing passages in the funerary rite.
- How Cael obtained and concealed his copies after being expelled.
- Whether Cael still considers himself a priest or retains any private faith.
