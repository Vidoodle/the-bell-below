# Dame Riona Voss

**Status:** Working canon

Riona is a paladin of Grayhaven's civic order. She grew up believing the public account of Saint Orra's sacrifice and joined the order when she came of age. She has spent her adult life fulfilling an oath to protect Grayhaven's people.

## Faith and duty

Riona trusts the council, church, and city watch because she has served beside them and seen them carry out their duties. Her faith is sincere rather than naive: she knows institutions and individuals can fail, but she believes their offices exist to serve Grayhaven.

Riona's oath is to the city's people, not merely to whoever gives her orders. She has not yet had reason to distinguish between those obligations. What she learns inside the cathedral will force that distinction, but the player determines how she resolves it.

## The Bell-Warden

After the Bell's first toll, the council gives Riona the temporary office of Bell-Warden. Her sealed commission directs her to enter the drowned cathedral and stop the Bell before midnight. It does not contain a hidden order to preserve the Bell or conceal what she finds.

The city watch has orders to admit Riona at the Breach Stair and assist her if asked. She is the only protagonist with automatic lawful access to the cathedral.

## What Riona knows

Riona knows the public account of Saint Orra's miracle and the rumors that the flood-dead remain behind the cathedral's seals. She does not know the refugees were deliberately sacrificed, that the church altered the history of the siege, or what ending the binding will cost.

## Unresolved

- Riona's age, family, and path into the civic order.
- The civic order's structure, practices, and relationship with the church and council.
- Whether Riona's paladin abilities are explicitly supernatural.
- What authority the office of Bell-Warden grants beyond access to the Breach Stair.
