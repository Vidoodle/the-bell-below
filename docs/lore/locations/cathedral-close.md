# The Cathedral Close

**Status:** Working canon

The Cathedral Close is the walled religious precinct at the foot of the Drowned Stair. It includes the cathedral courtyard, clergy buildings, and the exterior approaches to the cathedral itself. Before the siege, the stair delivered worshippers directly into this precinct.

The close remains flooded with the rest of the abandoned Lower Ward. The cathedral rises from a raised foundation within it, but the surrounding courtyard and lesser buildings stand in black water. The protagonist enters the close from the lower landing of the Drowned Stair before reaching the cathedral doors.

The close is a separate playable location, not transitional scenery. It will support its own exploration, situations, hazards, and consequences before the protagonist enters the Drowned Nave. Its specific occupants, discoveries, accessible buildings, and routes remain to be designed rather than assumed.
