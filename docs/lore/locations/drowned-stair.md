# The Drowned Stair

**Status:** Working canon

## Place

The Drowned Stair was once the ceremonial approach from higher Grayhaven into the Lower Ward. Its broad stone steps descended from an upper landing into the cathedral close, a walled precinct containing the courtyard, clergy buildings, and the cathedral's main entrance.

The stair is a single playable location comprising the upper landing, the post-siege gate, the descent, and the lower landing. [The Cathedral Close](cathedral-close.md) begins at its foot as a separate playable location.

## Flood and abandonment

During the siege, raiders broke a tide gate at low water and entered the Lower Ward along the exposed channel. The returning tide poured through the damaged gate and flooded the district.

Grayhaven rebuilt its defenses farther inland and left the Lower Ward outside the new retaining wall. The city did not drain or reclaim it. Broken tidal works and seepage keep the abandoned district inundated: the upper flights of the stair remain dry, while the lower steps descend into black water. The cathedral stands on a raised foundation, leaving its doors and much of the Nave accessible above the deeper water.

Publicly, the city treated the Lower Ward as unstable ground and a mass grave. Privately, leaving it flooded and closed helped the council and church conceal the refugees' sacrifice, the undead within the cathedral, and the truth of Saint Orra's miracle.

## Seal and access

The new retaining wall crosses the stair's upper landing. A fortified gate there preserves the only intact landward route into the cathedral close. The other streets that once entered the Lower Ward were walled off, collapsed, or lost beneath the flood; there is no convenient unguarded route that bypasses this entrance.

For forty years the gate remained barred under church seal and city-watch guard. After the Bell sounds again, the council orders it opened and the watch establishes a cordon across the upper landing. A watch sergeant decides who may pass while a guard detail holds back the gathered crowd.

## Opening scene

The watch cordon is a scene at the Drowned Stair, not a separate location. The player's first challenge is gaining access through persuasion, deception, stealth, violence, or another credible approach. Riona has lawful entry, Seren can draw upon his standing with the watch, and Cael faces deliberate opposition from the church.

Once past the gate, the protagonist descends to the lower landing and enters the Cathedral Close.
