# Grayhaven

**Status:** Working canon

Grayhaven is an independent city-state on the coast, built across the islands, embankments, and tidal channels of a river delta. Its wealth comes from the river crossing, its harbor, and trade through the surrounding farmland.

Across a narrow sea lies a colder, rockier land whose seafaring clans cannot reliably grow enough food. Raids on Grayhaven's delta settlements for grain, livestock, timber, and metal are a recurring part of life on both shores. The raiders are human, and their violence is driven by subsistence rather than a desire to annex Grayhaven.

## Unresolved

- The name and internal culture of the nearby land and its raiding clans.
- The composition and authority of Grayhaven's council, church, civic order, and city watch.
- How much modern Grayhaven still depends on the farms and communities it failed during the siege.
