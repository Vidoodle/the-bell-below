# The Drowned Cathedral

**Status:** Working canon

The cathedral stands in Grayhaven's flooded Lower Ward. After the siege, the city sealed every known entrance and kept the site under guard. Rumors that the flood-dead still move inside have persisted despite the church's denials.

When the Bell of Mercy begins to toll, the council opens [the Drowned Stair](locations/drowned-stair.md) so an authorized agent can descend and stop it. This is the first sanctioned access to the cathedral in forty years.

The cathedral stands on a raised foundation within its flooded close. Its main doors and much of the Nave remain accessible, while its lower chambers lie deeper beneath the water.

## Unresolved

- The cathedral's original name and patron before it was renamed for Saint Orra.
- How much the council, church, and city watch each know about the undead below.
