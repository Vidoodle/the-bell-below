# The Drowned Cathedral

**Status:** Working canon

The cathedral stands in Grayhaven's flooded Lower Ward. After the siege, the city sealed every known entrance and kept the site under guard. Rumors that the flood-dead still move inside have persisted despite the church's denials.

When the Bell of Mercy begins to toll, the council orders the seal over the Breach Stair opened so an authorized agent can descend and stop it. The city watch controls the entrance. This is the first sanctioned access to the cathedral in forty years.

The opening creates a brief opportunity for anyone else who can persuade, deceive, evade, or overcome the guards. There is no convenient unguarded route into the cathedral; character knowledge may support different approaches to the Breach Stair without bypassing the first encounter.

## Unresolved

- The cathedral's original name and patron before it was renamed for Saint Orra.
- The physical layout of the Breach Stair and how the post-siege seal closed it.
- How much the council, church, and city watch each know about the undead below.
